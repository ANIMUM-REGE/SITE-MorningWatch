Privacy Policy

Morning Watch, by Animum Rege LLC. Last updated 2026-07-22.

Morning Watch collects nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. Morning Watch has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no servers of our own. We do not receive your reading history, your streak, your saved devotionals, your notes, your reminder times, or any personal information. The app makes no network connections of its own.

Where your data lives

Everything — your streak, saved devotionals, and notes — is stored only on your device. It is included in your device's normal backup, under your control. Deleting the app deletes the data.

Notifications

The optional daily reminder is scheduled entirely on your device. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

Payments

Morning Watch Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details. Manage or cancel anytime in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

Scripture

Passages are from the King James Version and the World English Bible, both in the public domain, bundled inside the app — no download, no lookup, no connection.

Children

Morning Watch is intended for adults and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the current version will always be at this address, with the date above updated.
