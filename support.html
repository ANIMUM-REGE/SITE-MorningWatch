Common questions

Is the daily devotional really free?

Yes. The daily devotional, your streak, saved entries, and one daily reminder are free, permanently. Pro adds the archive, reading plans, notes with export, extra reminders, and read-aloud.

How do I cancel Pro?

Subscriptions are managed by your store account, not by us: on iPhone, Settings → your name → Subscriptions; on Android, Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions. Canceling keeps Pro until the end of the paid period.

My reminder doesn't arrive

Check that notifications are allowed for Morning Watch in your phone's Settings, and that Do Not Disturb or Focus modes allow it. The reminder is scheduled on your device — no connection is needed.

Which Bible translations are used?

The King James Version and the World English Bible, both public domain. Switch in Settings → Scripture.
